Generates a warm minimalist studio portrait of a young woman with a square privacy block over the face for anonymized profile or concept imagery.

```
Create a realistic studio portrait photograph of a young East Asian woman from the upper chest up, framed vertically in a 3:4 crop. She has natural black hair styled in a loose low bun at the back, with soft bangs across the forehead area and a few wispy flyaway strands.
```
